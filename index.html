It’s Your Move.

The So What Movement is a business growth community for aspiring and established entrepreneurs.

We help people build stronger business foundations, improve their marketing, access coaching and strategic connections, and prepare for their next stage of growth.

Whether you are developing an idea, launching a business, or working to grow what you have already built, you can find practical support, structure, and community here.

About the Founder

Built by someone who understands the gap

The So What Movement was created by Curtis Lee Corbins, author, entrepreneur, transportation leader, and community advocate.

Through his work in transportation and through conversations with aspiring and established entrepreneurs, Curtis has seen how often people have strong ideas and real potential but still lack consistent guidance, marketing support, practical business resources, and the right connections.

The Movement was created to help people take the next step with more clarity, accountability, and support.

Building opportunities that move communities forward

Curtis’s work across entrepreneurship, transportation, and community support informs the practical focus of The So What Movement.

The Book by Curtis Lee Corbins

Life Be Lifing. So What?

A motivational guide for people building through real obstacles.

Life and business can bring setbacks, uncertainty, financial pressure, and moments that test your confidence. This book is for people who want to keep moving forward without pretending those challenges are not real.

Curtis Lee Corbins shares a message rooted in resilience, mindset, planning, and taking the next practical step. It is written to encourage entrepreneurs and anyone working toward a meaningful goal.

“Don’t Make Excuses. Make Moves.”

The MOVES Framework

M

Map out the blueprint

Create a clear direction for what you are building.

O

Observe and own the obstacles

Recognize what is in front of you and identify what you can address.

V

Visualize the victories

Stay connected to the progress and outcomes that matter to you.

E

Execute

Take intentional action, even when every detail is not perfect.

S

Scale and sustain

Build systems, habits, and support that can help you keep growing.

Quarterly Pitch Opportunity

The Big Move Fund

The Big Move Fund is a quarterly pitch opportunity for eligible Gold and Platinum members.

It is designed for entrepreneurs who are ready to present their business, explain their growth plans, receive feedback, and participate in a structured coaching process.

Selected participants may receive capital and ongoing coaching to support their growth plans. Funding terms may include an equity interest in the selected business.

Before You Apply

  • You must be an active Gold or Platinum member
  • You must maintain membership for at least three months
  • You must meet the official eligibility and application requirements
  • Funding is not guaranteed
  • Selected businesses may be required to participate in coaching
  • Funding terms may include equity
Timing: The next pitch competition is expected in December. Date and venue will be announced once confirmed.
